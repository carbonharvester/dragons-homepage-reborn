Supplier
Code.

The standards we hold ourselves and our Kenyan partners to. Applies to every supplier, contractor, and on-the-ground partner working under the Kapes Adventures name.

The headline: trips are led on the ground by Kenyans we contract directly. We do not subcontract programme delivery to UK or US tour operators. The Kenyans setting the agenda, leading the trip, and running the year-round work are our suppliers — and they are paid directly, fairly, and on time.

1 · Direct contracting

Programme delivery in Kenya is contracted directly from Kapes Adventures to Kenyan individuals and Kenyan-registered organisations. We do not route programme spend through Western intermediaries who take a margin and pass on a discounted rate.

2 · Fair pay

  • Trip leader day-rates are at or above the Kenya Wildlife Service / KATO benchmark for tour-leader fees.
  • Farm crew at Seeds2Education are paid year-round, not seasonally.
  • Community partners receive a fixed contribution per cohort, agreed in writing before the trip.
  • Payments are made within 14 days of invoice.

3 · Local sourcing

Food, accommodation, transport, and craft materials are sourced from Kenyan-owned businesses wherever possible. We avoid international hotel chains in favour of Kenyan-run eco-lodges and conservancies.

4 · Child labour and modern slavery

Suppliers must confirm they do not employ children under 18 in their direct workforce, and that they comply with local labour law on hours, wages, and conditions. This is a hard requirement; we audit by site visit before contracting.

5 · Environment

  • Conservation alignment. Where possible we work on or beside Kenyan conservation operations (Wildlife Works in the Kasigau corridor; Kenyan Wildlife Service partner sites).
  • Carbon-conscious manufacturing. Kapes Uniforms (sister brand) uses the world's first carbon-neutral garment factory. We tour cohorts through it as part of Community Conservation.
  • Single-use plastics. Trips do not provide bottled water; cohorts are issued reusable bottles refilled from a vetted on-site supply.

6 · Safeguarding alignment

All suppliers in contact with cohorts during a trip — drivers, lodge staff, activity leaders — are briefed on our Child Protection policy. Our Kenyan trip leaders are responsible for ensuring on-site adherence.

7 · Photo and media protocol

No supplier or contractor may distribute photos or video of trip participants — visiting students, Kenyan partners, or community members — without explicit consent and a signed media-release on file.

8 · Reporting concerns

Any supplier, contractor, employee, or community member who believes this code has been breached should contact the founder directly. Concerns are investigated, documented, and acted on. We will not retaliate against good-faith reports.
